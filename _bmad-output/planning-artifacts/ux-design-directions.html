Direction Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of the three design approaches.

A: Clean Canvas

  • Maximum graph real estate
  • Floating, translucent chrome
  • Detail as overlay card
  • Most immersive feel
  • Risk: floating UI can feel unanchored
  • Risk: overlay may obscure graph nodes

B: Split Pane

  • Always-visible detail panel
  • Status bar with full stats
  • Most structured, predictable
  • IDE-familiar for developers
  • Risk: panel wastes space when browsing
  • Risk: less graph space on smaller screens

C: Hybrid

  • Full canvas by default
  • Panel slides in on selection
  • Canvas pushes (not overlaps)
  • Best of both worlds
  • Risk: slight layout shift on panel open
  • Risk: more complex to implement
